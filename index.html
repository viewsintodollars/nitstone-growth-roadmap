02 · The strategy

Become Bengaluru's most local, most measurable gold-loan challenger, one branch catchment at a time.

Win the ground around each branch before any broad, multi-product push.

Days 1–90

Prove the engine

Fix compliance and tracking, win the nearest catchments, rebuild trust, find which channel, audience and message combinations disburse.

Days 91–180

Scale the engine

Grow winning catchments, add transfer, repeat and referral loops, and optimise media for disbursed value, not leads.

Days 181–270

Build authority

Turn six months of evidence into category authority, better lead scoring and repeat economics. Test adjacent products carefully.

Days 271–360

Build the expansion platform

Budget by marginal disbursal value, document the new-branch system, and decide: deeper Bengaluru, more branches or a new city.

Each phase has three 30-day milestones, each with a data gate. Every 90 days: scale, optimise or stop.

07 · The borrower's journey

Seven steps from needing money to coming back.

01

Need

"I need money quickly, but I do not want to sell my gold."

It needsLocal-language stories: when a gold loan helps, what it costs, why trust Nitstone

02

Search

"How much can I get? What is the rate? Is my gold safe? Which lender is near me?"

It needsBranch pages, an estimator, clear costs, safety proof, full Maps profiles

03

Compare

"Can I trust this company with my gold?"

It needsOne regulated identity, genuine reviews, staff on camera, real complaint replies

04

Contact

"Can someone answer now and tell me exactly what to bring?"

It needsSticky call and WhatsApp, pincode routing, reply inside five minutes

05

Commit

"Is the trip worth making?"

It needsNamed officer, map and hours, confirmation, document list, reminder

06

Disburse

"Is the valuation fair and the agreement transparent?"

It needsClear valuation, KFS and APR, custody explained, the 30 minutes timed

07

Return

"Can I repay, top up, renew or retrieve my gold easily?"

It needsReminders, service WhatsApp, top-up and renewal, review and referral asks

Competitors have invested most in step seven. That's where acquisition cost compounds into lifetime value.

10 · Organic, with a job to do

Organic isn't decoration. It has two commercial jobs.

Job 1

Make paid ads convert

Someone sees an ad and checks your profile, reviews, branch listing, website or YouTube. What they find has to make you feel safe. That lifts paid conversion and cuts wasted spend.

Bring in borrowers on its own

Maps, local SEO, searchable videos, referrals, repeat-customer messages and useful social posts should generate calls, WhatsApp chats, branch visits and disbursals by themselves.

10 · From a Reel to a disbursal

Organic content with a conversion path, measured like performance.

  1. A Kannada Reel asks: "Why can two 20-gram ornaments get different gold-loan values?"
  2. The profile's pinned posts show the 30-minute process, branches and gold safety.
  3. The Reel offers something useful: "Comment VALUE for the valuation checklist" or "WhatsApp your pincode".
  4. The reply sends the checklist, asks permission, captures pincode and language, offers the nearest branch.
  5. Salesforce logs the source, content, need state and branch.
  6. The branch officer confirms documents, time and place.
  7. Visit, application and disbursal are credited back to the content theme.
